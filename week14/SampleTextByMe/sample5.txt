The software platform grew rapidly without a scalable architecture, leading to fragile dependencies where small updates often break unrelated features. Performance degrades during traffic spikes due to inconsistent caching and slow database queries caused by missing indexes. The machine learning pipeline is unreliable because of potential data leakage and poor reproducibility, specifically failing to track random seeds and hyperparameters. Security is also compromised since credentials are left in configuration files and backups are never tested for restoration. Although leadership recognizes the need for refactoring, tight deadlines are preventing progress, causing technical debt to keep accumulating.
