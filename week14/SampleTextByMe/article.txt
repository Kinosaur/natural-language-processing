Large language models are changing NLP because they can generate and understand text at near human level, but they need huge datasets, lots of computation, and strong evaluation to be trustworthy. The problem is that current evaluation methods are weak. Benchmarks can be outdated or biased, automated metrics like BLEU and ROUGE don’t really measure true meaning or quality, and human evaluations are inconsistent. Prompt engineering also affects results a lot, but prompts are often not documented, which hurts reproducibility. So the field needs better evaluation standards and clearer reporting practices.
