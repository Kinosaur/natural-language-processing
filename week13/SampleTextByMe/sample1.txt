Yesterday I was thinking about how overwhelming technology feels because it changes so fast, making things like CDs outdated while apps and devices constantly demand updates. Companies keep releasing "new" products that are actually barely different, and people complain about privacy even though they still overshare their personal lives on social media. At work, emails are becoming too long and unclear, which leads to unnecessary meetings that waste time. On top of that, news headlines are dramatic but not clear, so staying informed feels mentally exhausting.
