The current microservice architecture is suffering from latency spikes during peak traffic, likely caused by inefficient load balancing and legacy synchronous calls that block requests. The database is also performing poorly on complex joins because of outdated statistics or missing composite indexes. Debugging these issues is difficult because the logging is fragmented across different tools and lacks consistent structure. Furthermore, the CI/CD pipeline often fails due to dependency version drift between environments, and recent schema changes were pushed without full regression tests.
