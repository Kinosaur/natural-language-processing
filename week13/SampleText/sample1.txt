Yesterday I was thinking about how technology keeps changing everything so fast like one moment we’re using CDs and the next moment nobody even remembers what a CD looks like and honestly it feels overwhelming because every app wants an update and every device wants a new version and companies keep releasing “revolutionary” products that are basically the same as last year’s but with a slightly better camera or a new color and at the same time people complain about privacy but still share their personal lives on social media including what they ate for breakfast or where they’re traveling next week and meanwhile emails keep piling up at work because nobody knows how to write concise messages anymore so every email becomes a mini essay with unclear action items and multiple people copied unnecessarily and meetings get scheduled to discuss problems that could have been solved in five minutes if someone had just written a clear summary and on top of that the news is filled with headlines that are dramatic but vague so readers have to dig through long articles just to understand what actually happened which makes it hard to stay informed without feeling mentally exhausted by the constant flow of information.
